# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**Landlord:** Harborview Properties LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Landlord")
**Tenant:** Brightwater Bakery Co., an Oregon corporation ("Tenant")
**Premises:** Suite 210, 4400 Alder Street, Portland, Oregon 97205
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Lease") is made between Landlord and Tenant as of the Effective Date. The parties agree as follows.

---

## ARTICLE 1. BASIC LEASE TERMS

1.1 **Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, the space known as Suite 210 on the second floor of the building at 4400 Alder Street, Portland, Oregon (the "Building"). The Premises contain approximately 2,150 rentable square feet, as shown on Exhibit A.

1.2 **Term.** The term of this Lease is sixty (60) months, beginning on March 1, 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and ending on the last day of February 2030, unless ended earlier or extended under this Lease.

1.3 **Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises only as a retail bakery and cafe, including the baking, display and sale of baked goods, coffee and related beverages, and light office use that supports that business. Tenant may not use the Premises for any other purpose without Landlord's written consent.

1.4 **Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent according to the schedule below. Rent for any partial month is prorated by the number of days.

| Lease Year | Period | Monthly Base Rent | Annual Base Rent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mar 2025 - Feb 2026 | $4,300.00 | $51,600.00 |
| 2 | Mar 2026 - Feb 2027 | $4,429.00 | $53,148.00 |
| 3 | Mar 2027 - Feb 2028 | $4,561.87 | $54,742.44 |
| 4 | Mar 2028 - Feb 2029 | $4,698.72 | $56,384.64 |
| 5 | Mar 2029 - Feb 2030 | $4,839.68 | $58,076.16 |

1.5 **Security Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall pay Landlord a security deposit of $8,600.00 (equal to two months of first-year Base Rent), to be held under Article 5.

1.6 **Parking.** Tenant may use up to four (4) unreserved parking spaces in the Building's lot at no added charge. Tenant's customers may use the lot's visitor spaces, which are shared with other tenants.

1.7 **Notice Addresses.** Notices to Landlord go to Harborview Properties LLC, 900 Commerce Way, Suite 400, Portland, Oregon 97204, Attention: Property Manager. Notices to Tenant go to the Premises, Attention: Owner. Either party may change its address by notice to the other.

## ARTICLE 2. RENT AND OTHER PAYMENTS

2.1 **Payment of Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in advance, on or before the first day of each calendar month, without demand and without deduction or setoff, except where this Lease says otherwise. Tenant shall pay by electronic transfer to the account Landlord names in writing, or by any other method Landlord accepts in writing.

2.2 **Late Charge.** If Landlord has not received any payment of rent within five (5) days after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late fee of five percent (5%) of the overdue amount. If any payment of rent is more than fifteen (15) days late, the unpaid amount also bears interest at the rate of one percent (1%) per month from the due date until paid. The parties agree that the late fee is a reasonable estimate of Landlord's added cost of handling a late payment and is not a penalty. Landlord's acceptance of a late fee does not waive any other right of Landlord.

2.3 **Returned Payments.** If a payment is returned unpaid for any reason, Tenant shall pay Landlord a returned-payment fee of $45.00 for each return, plus any late fee that applies. After two returned payments in any twelve-month period, Landlord may require Tenant to pay all later rent by certified funds.

2.4 **Operating Expenses.** In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its Proportionate Share of Operating Expenses. Tenant's Proportionate Share is 8.4 percent, which is the Premises' rentable area divided by the Building's rentable area. "Operating Expenses" means the reasonable costs of owning, operating, cleaning, insuring and maintaining the Building and its common areas, including utilities for common areas, landscaping, snow removal, common-area repairs, property management fees not above four percent (4%) of gross receipts, and insurance premiums. Operating Expenses do not include: (a) debt payments or ground rent; (b) costs of leasing space to other tenants, including commissions and legal fees; (c) capital replacements, except the yearly cost of a capital item that reduces Operating Expenses, spread over its useful life; (d) costs Landlord recovers from insurance or from another tenant; (e) fines or penalties caused by Landlord's failure to follow the law; and (f) Landlord's income taxes.

2.5 **Estimated Payments and Yearly Reconciliation.** Landlord shall estimate Operating Expenses for each calendar year and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate with each monthly payment of Base Rent. For the first year the estimate is $1,075.00 per month. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall send Tenant a statement of the actual Operating Expenses. If Tenant has paid too little, Tenant shall pay the difference within thirty (30) days after the statement. If Tenant has paid too much, Landlord shall credit the difference against the next payments, or refund it in the last year of the term.

2.6 **Audit Right.** Within ninety (90) days after Tenant receives a yearly statement, Tenant may, on ten (10) business days' notice, inspect Landlord's records of Operating Expenses for that year during normal business hours. If the inspection shows Landlord charged Tenant more than 5 percent too much, Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the inspection.

2.7 **Taxes.** Tenant shall pay, before they are late, all taxes on Tenant's equipment, fixtures, inventory and personal property. Real property taxes for the Building are part of Operating Expenses, and Tenant pays its Proportionate Share.

2.8 **Utilities.** Tenant shall pay for electricity, gas, water, sewer, internet and telephone service used at the Premises. If a utility is not separately metered, Landlord shall fairly divide the cost, and Tenant shall pay its share within twenty (20) days after Landlord's bill. Landlord is not responsible for any stop in a utility service that Landlord does not cause.

2.9 **Application of Payments.** Landlord may apply any payment first to late fees and interest, then to older charges, and last to current Base Rent, whatever Tenant writes on the payment.

## ARTICLE 3. USE OF THE PREMISES

3.1 **Compliance with Law.** Tenant shall follow all laws, permits and rules that apply to Tenant's business and use of the Premises, including food-safety, health, fire and accessibility laws. Tenant is responsible for getting and keeping every license and permit its business needs, including a food-service license from the county health department.

3.2 **Hours.** Tenant may operate at any hours the law allows. Because the Building has other tenants, Tenant shall not start deliveries or loud baking-equipment cleaning before 5:00 a.m. or after 10:00 p.m.

3.3 **Odors, Noise and Waste.** Tenant shall keep cooking and baking odors, noise and vibration from disturbing other tenants. Tenant shall install and maintain, at its own cost, the ventilation and grease-control equipment that Exhibit B requires. Tenant shall not pour grease or oil into the Building's drains. Tenant shall keep all food waste in sealed containers and shall move them to the waste enclosure at least once each day.

3.4 **Signs.** Tenant may place one sign at the Building entrance and one sign on the Premises' door, if the signs follow the Building's sign standards and the law. Tenant needs Landlord's written consent for the design before it installs a sign, and shall remove its signs and repair any damage when the Lease ends.

3.5 **Pets and Animals.** No animals may be kept in the Premises or the Building, except (a) service animals that help a person with a disability, as the law requires, and (b) as set out in this Section. Landlord may agree in writing to let Tenant's staff or customers bring a pet to an outdoor seating area. If Landlord agrees to any pet in the Premises, Tenant shall pay a pet deposit of $750.00 for each animal before the animal arrives. Landlord shall hold the pet deposit apart from the security deposit and may use it to pay for cleaning, deodorizing and repair of damage that an animal causes. Landlord shall refund what is left of the pet deposit within thirty (30) days after the animal leaves for good or the Lease ends, whichever is first. Tenant is responsible for any injury or damage an animal causes.

3.6 **No Nuisance.** Tenant shall not do or allow anything in the Premises that is dangerous, is a nuisance, increases Landlord's insurance cost, or blocks the Building's shared hallways, stairs or loading area.

3.7 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous material into the Building, except ordinary cleaning products and the fuels, gases and supplies a bakery normally uses, in small amounts and in the way the law requires. Tenant shall pay to clean up any release of hazardous material that Tenant or its staff, suppliers or customers cause.

3.8 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall follow the Building's written rules, as Landlord reasonably updates them. If a rule conflicts with this Lease, this Lease controls.

3.9 **Access by Landlord.** Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at reasonable times, after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice when possible, to inspect, to make repairs, or to show the Premises to buyers, lenders or, during the last six months of the term, to future tenants. Landlord may enter at once in an emergency. Landlord shall try to avoid disrupting Tenant's business.

## ARTICLE 4. REPAIRS, MAINTENANCE AND ALTERATIONS

4.1 **Landlord's Duties.** Landlord shall keep in good repair the roof, foundation, outer walls, structure, common areas, and the main plumbing, electrical and heating, ventilation and air-conditioning ("HVAC") systems that serve more than one tenant. Landlord shall begin a repair that is Landlord's duty within a reasonable time after Tenant's notice, and shall begin at once for an emergency.

4.2 **Tenant's Duties.** Tenant shall keep the inside of the Premises clean, safe and in good order, and shall repair damage caused by Tenant or its staff, suppliers or customers. Tenant is responsible, at its own cost, for: (a) all of its ovens, mixers, refrigeration and other equipment; (b) the grease trap and the drain lines that serve only the Premises; (c) interior glass, doors and locks; (d) light bulbs and fixtures; (e) pest control inside the Premises; and (f) the HVAC unit that serves only the Premises, including a service visit twice each year by a licensed company and delivery of the service record to Landlord on request.

4.3 **Alterations.** Tenant shall not make any change, addition or improvement to the Premises (an "Alteration") without Landlord's written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold or delay. Landlord may refuse consent for any Alteration that affects the Building's structure or systems or can be seen from outside the Premises. Tenant shall make every Alteration with licensed contractors, with the permits the law requires, and in a good and careful way. Tenant shall keep the Premises free of liens for work done for Tenant, and shall remove any lien within twenty (20) days after it is filed.

4.4 **Tenant Improvement Allowance.** Landlord shall pay up to $21,500.00 (the "Allowance"), which is $10.00 per rentable square foot, toward the cost of Tenant's first build-out of the Premises under the plans in Exhibit B. Landlord shall pay the Allowance in one payment, within thirty (30) days after Tenant (a) opens for business, (b) delivers final lien waivers from every contractor, and (c) delivers a copy of the certificate of occupancy. Tenant shall pay every cost above the Allowance. Tenant must use the Allowance within the first twelve (12) months of the term or lose any unused part.

4.5 **Ownership of Improvements.** All Alterations that are fixed to the Premises become Landlord's property when the Lease ends, unless Landlord asks Tenant in writing to remove them. Tenant's movable equipment, furniture, trade fixtures and inventory remain Tenant's property, and Tenant shall remove them when the Lease ends.

4.6 **Condition at the End.** At the end of the term, Tenant shall give back the Premises clean and in good order, except for normal wear and tear, damage by fire or other casualty not caused by Tenant, and repairs that are Landlord's duty. Tenant shall repair any damage caused by moving out.

## ARTICLE 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

5.1 **Holding the Deposit.** Landlord shall hold the security deposit as security for Tenant's duties under this Lease. Landlord may keep the deposit in its general funds. The deposit earns no interest for Tenant. The deposit is not a limit on what Tenant may owe, and Tenant may not use it to pay the last month's rent.

5.2 **Use of the Deposit.** Landlord may use the deposit, in whole or in part, to pay unpaid rent, to repair damage beyond normal wear and tear, to clean the Premises, or to cover any other cost caused by Tenant's breach. If Landlord uses any of the deposit during the term, Tenant shall pay Landlord enough, within ten (10) days after Landlord's notice, to restore the full amount.

5.3 **Return of the Deposit.** Within forty-five (45) days after the Lease ends and Tenant has given back the Premises, Landlord shall return what is left of the deposit, with a written list of each amount kept and why.

5.4 **Transfer.** If Landlord sells the Building, Landlord may transfer the deposit to the buyer, and then Landlord has no more duty for it once the buyer agrees in writing to hold it under this Lease.

## ARTICLE 6. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

6.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** During the term, Tenant shall carry at its own cost: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 for each event and $2,000,000 in total each year; (b) property insurance for the full replacement cost of Tenant's equipment, inventory and improvements; (c) workers' compensation insurance as the law requires; and (d) business interruption insurance for at least six months of Base Rent and Operating Expenses. Tenant's liability policy shall name Landlord and Landlord's lender as additional insured parties. Tenant shall give Landlord a certificate of insurance before the Commencement Date, and again at each renewal of the policies.

6.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall carry property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost, and liability insurance for the common areas. The cost is part of Operating Expenses.

6.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives any claim against the other for loss or damage that is covered by the waiving party's property insurance, even if the other party's fault caused it, to the extent the insurance pays. Each party shall make sure its policies allow this waiver.

6.4 **Indemnity by Tenant.** Tenant shall defend and pay for, and hold Landlord harmless from, any claim, loss or cost (including reasonable lawyers' fees) that comes from (a) Tenant's use of the Premises, (b) any breach of this Lease by Tenant, or (c) the fault of Tenant or its staff, suppliers or customers, except to the extent Landlord's fault caused it.

6.5 **Indemnity by Landlord.** Landlord shall defend and pay for, and hold Tenant harmless from, any claim, loss or cost (including reasonable lawyers' fees) that comes from Landlord's fault or from any breach of this Lease by Landlord, except to the extent Tenant's fault caused it.

6.6 **No Liability for Some Losses.** Landlord is not responsible for loss of or damage to Tenant's property from theft, fire, water, or other causes that Landlord does not cause by its fault. Neither party is responsible to the other for lost profits or other indirect losses, except as Article 10 allows.

## ARTICLE 7. DAMAGE AND CONDEMNATION

7.1 **Damage.** If fire or another casualty damages the Premises or the Building, Tenant shall tell Landlord at once. If the Premises can be repaired within one hundred eighty (180) days, Landlord shall repair them with reasonable speed and Base Rent and Operating Expenses are reduced in proportion to the part of the Premises Tenant cannot use, until the repair is done.

7.2 **Right to End.** If the repair will take more than one hundred eighty (180) days, or if the damage happens in the last twelve (12) months of the term, either party may end this Lease by notice to the other within thirty (30) days after the casualty. If a casualty is caused by Tenant's fault, Tenant has no right to a rent reduction or to end the Lease for it.

7.3 **Condemnation.** If a government takes all of the Premises by eminent domain, or a part that makes the Premises unfit for Tenant's use, this Lease ends on the date of the taking. Each party may claim its own award. Tenant may also claim any award for moving costs and for Tenant's trade fixtures.

## ARTICLE 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 **Consent Needed.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease, sublet any part of the Premises, or let another person occupy them, without Landlord's written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold consent. It is reasonable for Landlord to refuse if the new user has a weaker financial position than Tenant, would use the Premises in a way that this Lease does not allow, or would add significant burden to the Building.

8.2 **Request.** A request for consent must include the new user's name, business, financial statements and the main terms of the deal. Landlord shall answer within twenty (20) days after receiving a complete request.

8.3 **Change of Control.** A sale of more than half of Tenant's ownership counts as an assignment. A sale of the bakery business to a buyer who keeps the same use does not need Landlord's consent if the buyer has at least the same net worth as Tenant and Tenant gives Landlord thirty (30) days' notice.

8.4 **No Release.** Even with Landlord's consent, Tenant stays responsible for the Lease, unless Landlord releases Tenant in writing.

8.5 **Sharing of Profit.** If Tenant sublets for more rent than Tenant pays Landlord, Tenant shall pay Landlord half of the extra rent, after Tenant recovers its reasonable costs of the sublease.

## ARTICLE 9. RENEWAL OPTION AND ENDING THE LEASE

9.1 **Option to Renew.** Tenant may renew this Lease for one (1) further term of sixty (60) months, on the same terms except for Base Rent, if (a) Tenant is not in default when it gives notice and when the renewal begins, and (b) Tenant gives Landlord written notice of renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days, but not more than three hundred sixty-five (365) days, before the term ends. If Tenant misses this notice period, the option ends.

9.2 **Rent for the Renewal Term.** Base Rent for the first year of the renewal term is the greater of (a) 103 percent of the last monthly Base Rent, or (b) the fair market rent for similar space in the area. The parties shall agree on fair market rent within sixty (60) days after Tenant's notice. If they do not agree, each shall name one licensed commercial appraiser. The two appraisers shall pick a third. The third appraiser shall choose the figure, from one of the first two appraisers' figures, that is nearer to fair market rent. Base Rent then rises by 3 percent each year of the renewal term.

9.3 **Early Termination by Tenant.** Tenant may end this Lease early, effective at the end of the thirty-sixth (36th) month of the term, by giving Landlord at least ninety (90) days' written notice and paying Landlord, with the notice, a termination fee equal to four (4) months of Base Rent at the rate then in effect, plus the unamortized part of the Allowance and of Landlord's leasing costs, spread evenly over sixty months.

9.4 **Notice of Vacating.** If Tenant does not renew, Tenant shall give Landlord written notice at least sixty (60) days before the term ends that it will move out. Tenant shall give notice of the day it will give back the Premises. If Tenant keeps the Premises after the term ends without Landlord's written consent, Tenant becomes a tenant from month to month at a monthly Base Rent equal to 150 percent of the last Base Rent, and either party may end that tenancy by giving the other at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.5 **Landlord's Notice to Change or End.** For Landlord to end a month-to-month tenancy, or to change its terms, Landlord must give Tenant at least thirty (30) days' written notice. A longer notice period of ninety (90) days applies to a termination of this Lease by Landlord for redevelopment of the Building, which Landlord may do only after the thirty-sixth month, and Landlord shall then pay Tenant's reasonable moving costs, up to $12,000.00.

9.6 **Surrender.** On the last day of the term, Tenant shall return all keys and access cards, and shall remove all of its property. Landlord may treat any property Tenant leaves for more than ten (10) days after the term ends as abandoned and may dispose of it at Tenant's cost.

## ARTICLE 10. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

10.1 **Tenant's Default.** Each of these is a default by Tenant: (a) Tenant fails to pay rent or any other payment when due and does not pay within five (5) days after Landlord's written notice; (b) Tenant fails to do anything else this Lease requires and does not fix the problem within thirty (30) days after Landlord's written notice, or, if the problem cannot be fixed in thirty days, does not begin within that time and finish with reasonable speed; (c) Tenant leaves the Premises empty for more than sixty (60) days in a row while it still owes rent; or (d) Tenant becomes bankrupt or insolvent, or makes an assignment for the benefit of its creditors.

10.2 **Landlord's Remedies.** If Tenant is in default, Landlord may, by notice: (a) end this Lease and re-enter the Premises as the law permits; (b) keep the Lease in effect and collect rent as it falls due; or (c) do whatever Tenant failed to do, at Tenant's cost, and Tenant shall pay Landlord's costs, plus 10 percent for overhead, within ten (10) days after demand. Landlord shall try in good faith to re-let the Premises to reduce Tenant's loss, as the law requires. These remedies are cumulative.

10.3 **Damages.** If Landlord ends this Lease for Tenant's default, Tenant shall pay Landlord (a) all rent and charges unpaid up to the end date, (b) the cost of getting the Premises ready for a new tenant and of commissions for re-letting, and (c) the amount by which the rent for the rest of the term exceeds the rent Landlord actually receives or reasonably could receive from a new tenant.

10.4 **Landlord's Default.** Landlord is in default if it fails to do something this Lease requires and does not fix the problem within thirty (30) days after Tenant's written notice, or, if it takes longer, does not start within that time and finish with reasonable speed. If Landlord is in default, Tenant may fix the problem and charge Landlord the reasonable cost, or may use any other remedy the law gives. If a Landlord default stops Tenant from using a major part of the Premises for more than ten (10) days in a row, Base Rent is reduced in proportion to the lost use until it is fixed.

10.5 **Lawyers' Fees.** In any court case or arbitration about this Lease, the winning party may recover its reasonable lawyers' fees and costs from the other party.

10.6 **Waiver.** If a party does not insist on a strict following of a term of this Lease, that does not waive the term. A waiver must be in writing and signed.

## ARTICLE 11. GENERAL PROVISIONS

11.1 **Quiet Enjoyment.** If Tenant pays rent and follows this Lease, Tenant may use the Premises in peace during the term, without interference from Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

11.2 **Subordination.** This Lease is below any mortgage that Landlord puts on the Building, but only if the lender agrees in writing not to disturb Tenant's use while Tenant is not in default. Tenant shall sign a statement within fifteen (15) days after Landlord's request confirming that the Lease is in force and stating its main terms.

11.3 **Notices.** A notice under this Lease must be in writing. It is given when delivered by hand, one business day after being sent by a national overnight carrier, or three business days after being sent by certified mail with return receipt, to the address in Section 1.7. Notices of rent or other money matters may also be sent by email, if the receiving party has given an email address for that purpose in writing.

11.4 **Force Majeure.** A party is not in default for a delay in doing something other than paying money, if the delay is caused by a strike, a natural disaster, a government order, or any other event outside the party's reasonable control. The party shall tell the other quickly and shall act with reasonable speed once the event ends.

11.5 **Governing Law.** The laws of the State of Oregon govern this Lease. Any court case about this Lease shall be filed in the state or federal courts that serve Multnomah County, Oregon, and each party agrees to their authority.

11.6 **Dispute Resolution.** Before any court case (except a case for the quick return of the Premises or for a court order to stop harm), the parties shall meet within fifteen (15) days after one gives the other written notice of a dispute and try in good faith to settle it. If they cannot settle it within forty-five (45) days after the notice, either may start a case.

11.7 **Entire Agreement.** This Lease, with its Exhibits, is the whole agreement of the parties about the Premises. It replaces all earlier talks and writings. A change to this Lease must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.8 **Severability.** If a court finds any part of this Lease not enforceable, the rest of the Lease stays in force.

11.9 **Binding Effect.** This Lease binds and helps the parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

11.10 **Brokers.** Each party states that it dealt with no broker in this lease, except Cascade Commercial Advisors, whose fee Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement. Each party shall defend the other against any claim by any other broker that the first party caused.

11.11 **Guaranty.** Tenant shall deliver to Landlord, with the signed Lease, a personal guaranty in the form of Exhibit C, signed by Maria Okonkwo, the owner of Tenant. The guaranty ends after Tenant has paid all rent without a default for twenty-four (24) months in a row.

11.12 **Counterparts.** The parties may sign this Lease in separate copies, and electronic signatures count as original signatures.

11.13 **Time.** Time is important in this Lease. If a date for doing something falls on a Saturday, a Sunday or a legal holiday, the date moves to the next business day.

11.14 **Relationship of the Parties.** Nothing in this Lease makes the parties partners, joint venturers or employer and employee. Landlord has no share in Tenant's profits.

11.15 **Landlord's Liability.** Landlord's total liability to Tenant under this Lease is limited to Landlord's interest in the Building, including the money received from a sale, insurance or rent from it. Landlord's owners and managers have no personal liability.

11.16 **Confidentiality.** Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Lease private, except for what it must tell its lawyers, lenders, insurers, accountants or prospective buyers, or what the law requires.

11.17 **Holding Over by Landlord's Delay.** If Landlord cannot give Tenant the Premises by the Commencement Date, Base Rent and Operating Expenses do not begin until the day Landlord does. If Landlord has not delivered the Premises within ninety (90) days after the Commencement Date, Tenant may end this Lease by notice, and Landlord shall return all money Tenant has paid.

## ARTICLE 12. SPECIAL PROVISIONS

12.1 **Free Rent.** Landlord grants Tenant one month of free Base Rent for the first full month of the term (March 2025), if Tenant is not in default. Tenant still pays Operating Expenses for that month. If Tenant is in default at any time during the first twelve months and the default continues after the cure period, Tenant shall repay the free rent.

12.2 **Exclusive Use.** For as long as Tenant is open for business and not in default, Landlord shall not lease any other space in the Building to another tenant whose main business is the retail sale of baked goods or coffee. This does not prevent a restaurant or grocery store from selling those items as a small part of its business.

12.3 **Delivery Access.** Tenant may use the rear loading dock between 5:00 a.m. and 11:00 a.m. on weekdays for bread and ingredient deliveries, for no more than thirty (30) minutes at a time per delivery. Tenant shall not leave a delivery vehicle in the loading area at other times.

12.4 **Outdoor Seating.** With Landlord's written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, Tenant may place up to four tables on the sidewalk in front of the Building, if the city allows it and Tenant carries the added insurance for it. Tenant shall keep the area clean and shall remove the tables at night.

12.5 **Building Hours of Access.** The Building's main door is open from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. each day. Tenant's staff and suppliers will receive access cards for entry at other times. Lost access cards cost $25.00 each to replace.

12.6 **Trash and Recycling.** Landlord shall provide one shared waste container and one shared recycling container for the Building. If Tenant's waste is more than ordinary for an office tenant, Tenant shall pay for extra pickups, or arrange its own pickup and pay for it. Tenant shall break down all cardboard before putting it in the recycling container.

12.7 **Right of First Offer.** If Suite 212, next to the Premises, becomes open during the term, Landlord shall first offer it to Tenant on the terms Landlord is ready to accept from others. Tenant has ten (10) business days to accept. If Tenant does not accept, Landlord may lease it to anyone, but not on terms better for the other party than the terms offered to Tenant, for six (6) months.

12.8 **Pest Control.** Landlord shall hire a pest-control company to treat the common areas four times each year, at a cost that is part of Operating Expenses. Tenant is responsible for pest control inside the Premises and shall keep food in sealed containers.
