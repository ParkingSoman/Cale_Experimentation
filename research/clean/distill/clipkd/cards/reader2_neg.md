# Reader 2: negative poles (blind)

Read with `NEURON-CHECKLIST.md`. Each pattern is defined on the 12 TOP tiles and the 8 STRONG tiles shown. The sheets show only 8 of the 68 strong-band crops. The weak band is used only in the comparison column.

Crop-tool note: a crop with mirror symmetry (a V, chevron or butterfly shape) means the token sits at the photo border, and the crop tool padded it by reflection. Those shapes are NOT part of any pattern below. "Border" counts how many of the 20 boxes touch a photo edge or corner. These counts come from reading the thumbnails and are approximate.
