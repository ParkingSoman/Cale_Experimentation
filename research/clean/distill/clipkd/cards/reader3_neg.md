# Reader 3: negative poles (blind)

Source: the `*_neg.png` sheets only. Pattern defined on TOP (1-12) and STRONG (13-80) bands.
Tool note: many crops show a mirror image around their centre line. The red box on those tiles sits at the photo border, so the mirror is the crop tool's padding, not the photo. I read only the real half of those crops.
