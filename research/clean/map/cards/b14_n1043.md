# Block 14 n1043: soft margin of a close-up vs busy interior detail

## Distinction
- Positive: the left or right margin of a close-up photo. A soft, low-detail surface (shadow, defocus, the side of a big subject) that runs off the frame edge.
- Negative: busy, detailed content inside the frame (beads, petals, web, patterned plates, curled snakes).
- Distinction: "plain stuff cut by the photo's edge" against "detailed stuff in the open".

## Evidence
- Max 5.7, min -7.2. 70% of top-100 peaks sit in the border ring (chance 27%), mostly columns 0 and 13.
- Heat: red bands down the left and right sides; the central subject is blue.
- On-cells have less detail than other cells (gradient 10.6 vs 12.7) and are darker (59 vs 110). Darkness is not causal: dimming x0.3, brightening or inverting the top-20 images changes nothing (0.71-0.77 vs 0.75).
