# S8 early band: predictions written BEFORE the synthetic tests (2026-10-21)

| Neuron | Prediction |
|---|---|
| 1 n61 | Smoothness dial. A uniform patch (any gray or color) gives > 50% of max. Fine high-contrast texture (binary noise or checker, grain 2-4 px) gives a strong negative (< -50% of natural min). Coarse gratings (period 32) sit between. |
| 2 n332 | Both poles are flat. It reads the direction of a soft brightness slope, not brightness. One ramp direction > 50% of max; the opposite ramp negative; uniform patches near zero. |
| 3 n103 | Fine parallel vertical lines. A vertical square grating, period 4-8 px, > 70% of max. A horizontal grating < 10%. A blurred (period 32, soft) vertical grating gives a negative value. |
| 4 n187 | Line ends of thin dark strokes. The free end of a dark line gives > 2x the response at the middle of a long line. Text-like images > 50% of max. |
| 5 n373 | Oblique stripes at ONE diagonal (flip 0.67). A grating at one diagonal, period 8-16, > 50% of max; the mirror diagonal < 25%. |
| 2 n332 (2nd, after t332_local) | Faint 8x8 block grid detector: random 8x8 blocks +-3 gray > 50% of max; 4x4 and 16x16 much less; JPEG of a smooth ramp > the raw ramp. |
