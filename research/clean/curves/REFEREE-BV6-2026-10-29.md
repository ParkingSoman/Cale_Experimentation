## Verdict in one paragraph

The inequalities of Theorem TV18 and Proposition TV18.2 are correct, step by step, in both forms. The gains
dominate. The leak splits over the members. The pair assignment of TV18.2 is valid (it over-counts). Three things
are wrong around them. (1) Corollary TV18.1 part 3 says "M* is complete for every such zoom". The paper defines
completeness only through the carried leak claims of DM14 (Definition DM15). A map of member claims holds none, so
by the paper's own definition M* is complete for no zoom. Also, if every claim of M* has error 0, then N's centred
logits are 0 on P, so the exactness clause as written applies only to networks whose output does not change on P.
(2) The comment after TV18 says the bound "is 0 in the same exact case" as Corollary TV6.1. That is false. Member
claims cannot see cancellation between members inside D. (3) Proposition TV18.2 has a premise, eps_l + c^-_l > 0,
that is not in its tag and not on its path. Of the earlier results I read (TV1, TV6, TV6.1, TV6.2, TV7, TV12, TV16, TV17), none breaks. The fixes to BV45 findings 1 to 4, 6 and 6a are
in place; findings 3 and 6 leave small residue.

## Numbered findings

1. GAP (T5's exactness clause has no definition behind it). Corollary TV18.1 part 3: "M* is complete for every such
   zoom". Definition DM15 says that M is complete when it "holds carried leak claims (D_j, members, l) of DM14" for
   each level. M* holds member claims (DM16) only. So under DM15, M* is complete for no zoom. T5 makes its exactness
   promise only for maps "complete for the zoom (... defined in the paper)". So TV18.1 does not yet meet T5's
   exactness clause.
   Second, the premise of part 3 is degenerate. Claim (B, L + 1) has omega^B >= |P_perp U Q_B h_L| / nu_(L+1) >= 0. If
   every claim of M* has error 0, then P_perp U Q_B h_L = 0 on P for every member B. Sum over B: P_perp U h_L = 0 on
   P. So N's softmax output is the same at every point of P. Part 3 is true, but it covers only such networks.
   Fix: add a second clause to DM15. "A map M of member claims is complete for a zoom when, for every level j and
   every group S of G_j, M holds the claims (B, l), l = 1, ..., L + 1, of a set of members inside S of total
   dimension at least dim S - k_S." The rule's knapsack then runs over the members whose claims are in M. Part 3
   becomes: "If M is complete for the zoom and every claim of M has error 0, D_P(B_j) = 0." With this clause the
   premise is not degenerate, because M need not hold the claims of the members that are kept. A second form, for the
   full map M*: "if, in every group S of G_j, the members with s_B = 0 have total dimension at least dim S - k_S, the
   knapsack picks cost 0 and D_P(B_j) = 0." Say that M* is complete for every zoom under the new clause, and that its
   own exactness clause needs N's output to be constant on P.

2. ERROR (a false claim in the comment after TV18). "So the bound is looser than Corollary TV6.1's for any one D; it
   is 0 in the same exact case." The exact case of TV18 is strictly smaller than that of TV6.1. Counterexample, norm
   form, orthogonal members: D = B_1 + B_2, no reader reads D (R_l P_D = 0, U P_D = 0), |P_D h_(l-1)|^2 is the same at
   every point of P for every l, but |P_(B_1) h_(l-1)|^2 is not (the energy moves between B_1 and B_2). Some neuron
   of some block has r != 0, w != 0 and Lip_l > 0, and P_perp U != 0. Then TV6 part 3 gives every leak = 0, and
   omega = 0, so TV6.1's errors are 0 and the cut is exact (TV1 part 2). But mu^(B_1) for that neuron has the term
   |r| | |P_(B_1) h|^2 - c(B_1) | / nu^2 > 0, and in the norm form (u*^T M*_l)_A >= u*_A >= |P_perp U| / eps^(1/2) > 0
   for every A. So eps_mc(B_1, l) > 0 and TV18's bound is > 0. The same happens with reads: r . P_D h = 0 while
   r . P_(B_1) h = -r . P_(B_2) h != 0. An explicit instance: d = 3, B_1 = span(e_1), B_2 = span(e_2), K = span(e_3),
   D = B_1 + B_2; first write e(x) = (cos t_x, sin t_x, 1) with two values of t_x on P (cos^2 t_x not constant); one
   block of one neuron that reads e_3 and writes e_3 (Lip > 0); logits read e_3 (U with rows e_3^T and 0). The block
   writes only into e_3, so P_D h_l = P_D e(x) at every norm, abs(P_D h)^2 = 1 at every norm and point, and no reader
   reads D. TV1 part 2 gives D_P = 0 for the cut that drops D. But abs(P_(B_1) h)^2 = cos^2 t_x varies, so
   eps_mc(B_1, 1) > 0.
   This is the one structural fact in B.V.6, and it is not stated. It is the dual of Proposition TV17 part 2: there,
   per-area energies do not add up to the energy of D; here, the energy of D and the read of D do not split into

   exactness is strictly weaker than TV6.2's.

   0, which is strictly stronger than TV6.1's exact case", and state the counterexample as a remark or a part of
   TV18. Say plainly that zoom-free claims cost exactness where members cancel inside D.

## Answers to the brief's questions

Q1 (a). Yes, in both forms, for every sum of members D. Norm form, orthogonal members: Pi P_A = P_A or 0, so
|P_A Pi w| <= |P_A w|, |P_F Pi r| <= |P_F r|, |Pi r| <= |r|. c_l(D) = sum of c_l(B) >= 0, so nu'_l >= eps_l^(1/2) and
1 / rho_l <= 1 / eps_l^(1/2). No-norm form, any direct decomposition: Q Q_A = Q_A Q = Q_A or 0, so
|Q_A Q w| <= |Q_A w| and |Q_F^T Q^T r| = |(Q Q_F)^T r| <= |Q_F^T r|. The logit gain needs one more line in each form
(finding 7). The read representative needs a sentence (finding 8). Monotonicity of the products is correct (all
entries >= 0).

Q1 (b). Correct. The cut's read minus N's read is (r . Pi h)(1/nu' - 1/nu) - r . P_D h / nu.
|1/nu' - 1/nu| = |nu^2 - nu'^2| / (nu nu' (nu + nu')). With |r . Pi h| <= |r| |Pi h| <= |r| nu' (nu'^2 >= |Pi h|^2,
since eps_l + c_l(D) >= 0) and nu + nu' >= nu, the term is at most |r| |nu^2 - nu'^2| / nu^2. And
nu^2 - nu'^2 = |P_D h|^2 - c_l(D) = sum over B in D of (|P_B h|^2 - c_l(B)), by Pythagoras for orthogonal members and
its mean. At the logits |P_perp U Pi h| <= |P_perp U| nu', and the same steps give omega^B. Correct.

Q1 (c). Correct. Pointwise lambda_l[A] <= sum over B in D of lambda^B_l[A]; ||.|| is monotone on functions >= 0 and
obeys Minkowski. Then u^T M_l ||lambda_l|| <= u*^T M*_l sum_B ||lambda^B_l|| = sum_B eps_mc(B, l)^(1/2), and
||omega|| <= sum_B ||omega^B||. Squaring and Fact F11 (through TV6) finish it.

Q2. Each error is >= 0, is 0 exactly when its claim holds, and names no D, build, size or zoom. It is computed from N,
P and the whole member decomposition, not from the member alone (finding 5). It is computed to b bits in the
bit-model reading of DM11, as DM14 is. This meets T5 once the claim is written to name the decomposition.

Q3. The knapsack is right: costs s_B >= 0, integer weights dim B <= d, and the recursion
best(i, t) = min(best(i - 1, t), s_(B_i) + best(i - 1, max(t - dim B_i, 0))), with best(0, 0) = 0 and
best(0, t) = infinity for t > 0, is exact. Dropping all members of S is always feasible. The bound is separable over
groups and the square is monotone, so per-group minimisation minimises the bound. Switching is a lookup. The
overclaims are in part 3 (finding 1): "complete" is undefined for member claims, and its premise is degenerate.
"Least loss" is used only at loss 0, where it is trivially true; part 2 correctly says the bound, not the loss, is
least.

Q4. Energy: |h|^2 - |Q h|^2 = sum of g_(BF) over ordered pairs with B or F in D. Correct. c_l(D) >= c^-_l: diagonal
terms are >= 0, off-diagonal terms are >= their negative parts, and c^-_l sums the negative parts of a superset of
pairs. Correct. Assignment: member B in D receives (B, B), every (B, F) with F != B, and (F, B) with F in K; by
symmetry this is at most gamma^B_l. Correct (pairs inside D are counted twice in gamma, which is valid). Gains:
|Q^T r| <= sum over G in K of |Q_G^T r| <= s(r); |P_perp U Q| <= s(U); the other two dominations as in TV18. Correct.
Q^T r as the read: valid, because TV6's proof uses a read only through its action on K and its size; and Q^T r is in
D perp, so TV6's literal premise holds as well. The premise eps_l + c^-_l > 0 is a hidden restriction (finding 3).
"For orthogonal members every term is at least the matching term of DM16" is correct: c^- = 0, gamma^B reduces to
DM16's energy term, s(r) >= |r| and s(U) >= |P_perp U| by the triangle inequality.

Q5. Hidden restrictions: the TV18.2 premise (finding 3); the laminar premise of TV18.1's norm form is on its path,
but the S-laminar row text is stale (finding 12a). False statements: the comment after TV18 (finding 2). The
"more weakly" clause of DM16 is empty in the norm form (finding 6).
