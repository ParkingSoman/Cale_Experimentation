### TV6 (lines 4870-4930)

1. (OK) Step (a), lines 4895-4898. The identity n'(a') - n'(a) = (a' - a)/nu'(a) + a'(1/nu'(a') - 1/nu'(a)) is
   correct. nu' is 1-Lipschitz, since |nu'(a) - nu'(a')| <= ||a| - |a'|| <= |a - a'| (`nu_lip` plus the reverse
   triangle inequality). The factor |a'|/nu'(a') = |n'(a')| <= 1 is used correctly. The bound
   |r.(n'(a') - n'(a))| <= (|r.(a' - a)| + |r||a' - a|)/nu'(a) follows. The denominator is nu' at a = Pi h_(l-1),
   which is N's projected stream. That is why rho_l can be computed from N, D and the cut's eps' alone. This is the
   right choice.

2. (OK) Steps (b) and (c), lines 4899-4908. The leak split, the per-area split and the Lipschitz step are correct.
   delta_l = delta_(l-1) + Pi W_l (gt_l - g_l) holds because both sides carry Pi c_l. To replace 1/nu'_l(x) by 1/rho_l
   is valid only on P, and the theorem uses it only on P. All entries of T_l, lambda_l and u are >= 0, so the induction
   gives e_L <= sum_l M_l lambda_l. The index order of M_l is right: for L = 2, e_2 <= (I + T_2) lambda_1 + lambda_2.

3. (OK) Steps (d) and (e), lines 4909-4913. Step (d) is the matrix form of (a), applied with the rows of P_perp U'.
   |P_perp U' delta| <= sum_A |P_perp U' P_A| e_L[A] and |delta| <= sum_A e_L[A] give u^T e_L. The words "using
   |n'(.)| <= 1" add nothing here: the fact is already inside (a). Minkowski is applied to a sum of fixed nonnegative

### Conjecture DV2 (lines 7015-7023)

23. (GAP, the route has a false step) The coupling term is |r.n'(a')| |delta| / nu'(a). With
    |r.n'(a')| <= a + 2|r||delta| / nu'(a), the second-order term is 2|r||delta|^2 / rho^2. To bound it by
    a|delta| / rho you need |delta| <= a rho / (2|r|). The premise |delta| < rho / 4 does not give this when a << |r|,
    and that is the case the conjecture is about. At a = 0 the per-neuron form claimed by the conjecture fails. Take
    r . Pi h = 0 and a cut stream with nu'(ht) < nu'(Pi h) (delta with a component along -Pi h and one along r). The
    change is r.delta / nu'(ht), which then exceeds |r.delta| / rho, by a factor below 4/3 when |delta| <= rho / 4. The whole bound may survive through slack elsewhere,
    but the route does not show it. Weaker statement that is provable now (a lemma, not a conjecture):
    |r.(n'(a') - n'(a))| <= (|r.(a' - a)| + a |a' - a|) / nu'(a) + 2|r||a' - a|^2 / nu'(a)^2. Then TV6 holds with
    coupling a_(l,i) plus a quadratic term, under an explicit smallness premise on the stream error. Also, "if the
    leaks are small enough that the cut's stream error stays below rho_l / 4" is a condition on the cut's stream, not
    on the leaks. Write it as one.
