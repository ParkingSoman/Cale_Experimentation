## Findings by severity

1. **Part D paragraph overstates what is closed (lines 937-941).**
   a. Link 0 is not closed. The objective was switched from D_P to D2. T10.2 is an upper bound for one build. It has no lower bound. So the D2 optimum is not shown to be near the D_P optimum. The metric of D_P is diag(q) - q q^T. It can be far from P_perp when q is peaked.
   b. Links 2-4 come from T10 (H-eq, H-symN, Chat positive definite), not from T9. Link 4's "with no data" is not delivered: whether mode 0 is on top depends on chat(0) phihat(0), which is data.
   c. Link 9 is closed by T9, not by T7 (see T7 row).
   d. Link 7 is still needed. T10's frequency structure holds only for an exact H-eq and H-symN network. A real network needs a stability statement (Davis and Kahan on Chat^{1/2} Phi Chat^{1/2}).
   e. The class excludes per-unit ReLU (T1') and max-pool. D11 builds free every weight above the family. That is not the D6 bench of the table. A fold does not shrink the gate path (H-gate row). And by T10.1 the closure lies in a class where invariant outputs make the family fold to width 1.
   Fix: the provable link 0 below, plus a rewrite: "Inside the gated class, for the objective D2 and with free downstream weights, links 1, 8, 9 and 10 hold by T8, T9 and T7. Links 2-4 hold under H-eq and H-symN by T10. Link 0 holds up to the constant of Proposition X. Link 7 remains open."
   **Proposition X (proposed, provable now).** Suppose the range max_i y_i - min_i y_i of the teacher's logits is at most R almost surely. Then for every build B whose logits also have range at most R:
   - Var_q(v) >= q_min |P_perp v|^2, with q_min >= e^{-R} / n. This is because the q-mean is not the uniform minimiser of sum (v_i - c)^2.
   - The range is a seminorm, so it stays at most R on the whole segment y + t delta.
   - So KL >= (e^{-R} / (2n)) |P_perp delta|^2.
   - With F11 this gives D_P(B_D2) <= (n e^R / 2) times the infimum of D_P over builds of range at most R. Here B_D2 is the D2-optimal build of T9.
2. **D10's tree is vacuous. B4.1's headline is false under it (lines 193, 817).**
   Fix: define a tree with the child basis an orbit basis, made of rotated copies of one function under the cyclic group of that level. Then a two-line counting proof gives the tree failure at every level from 3 up, with no Fourier bound and no non-negativity:
   - An orbit basis of the degree-(d+1) space has a non-zero top coefficient.
   - So one child is never in the degree-d space, and each parent needs at least 2 children.
   - 2(2d + 1) > 2d + 3 for d >= 1.
   - For d = 0 it allows 1 -> 3, as part 1 says.
   Keep the Fejer argument as a separate and stronger statement ("not even one bump is in the cone"). Restrict it to equally spaced copies and to 5 dividing N, because the counterexample above shows that unequal copies admit the bump.
3. **H-gate does not say where the gates come from (line 276, D11, literature rows).** In every cited gated architecture, the gate of layer l+1 is a function of the value path of layer l. A build that keeps "the gate path" must keep that path at full width. Then the claim "a network folds exactly to any width" applies to the value path only.
   Fix: state in H-gate that the gates g_l are functions of x computed by a fixed map that the build does not change. Then restate the revision's claim as "the value path folds; the gate path is kept". Optionally add a definition of total width that counts the gate path.
4. **T9 quantifier (line 715).** Set k := min_l k_l and say the part-2 build has widths (k, ..., k), padded with zero units if needed. T10.2 inherits the fix.
5. **T10 part 5 (line 780).** Replace "the order of the numbers chat(n)" with "the numbers chat(n), through the order of lambda_n = chat(n) phihat(n)".
6. **T6 and T10 part 4: the ladder is for the canonical builds only (lines 758, 777).** State "the builds of T9 part 2 (resp. T10 part 3) form a fold ladder". The nesting of the spans needs exactly the two strict gaps. If the claim is meant for every optimal build, add the premise that the pooled covariance Sigma is positive definite on the span of the values of a(x, p), and fix the downstream edges to the identity.
