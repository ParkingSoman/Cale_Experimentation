## Answers to the six questions

1. B^0 on the clean table: correct (finding 7). The Cauchy-Schwarz conversions are correct. The kappa summaries for flat
   and shelter claims leave out factors (finding 9). The judgment step is correct, but it is not invariant under
   rescaling (finding 2).
2. Lower bound: it uses no fact about the teacher. L^0_S is the right object (finding 8).
3. Clean values: there is not always at most one clean value per entry. Two judgment claims can share a region, and a
   = 0 is open (finding 3). Blend and family errors have no clean values (finding 4). U is a product of intervals, as TE3
   part 3 needs (finding 17).
4. Hidden premises: a_R != 0 and one judgment claim per region (TE4); the fixed teacher and A_j > 0 (TE1 part 2); flat
   units against cover (TE4); h^0 for group members (TE4).
5. The comment after TE4 overclaims. TE4 does NOT meet T5's graded clause. Bounded swings of B^0 would not help: there
   is a complete map whose errors all go to 0 while D_P^U(B^0) - V_rob goes to infinity and V_rob - sum L^0_S stays
   bounded away from 0 (finding 1).
6. TM4 part 2 has the premise of TE1 part 2 (finding 14).

## The three most important fixes, ranked

1. **Say that the graded clause is not met, and prove it (finding 1).** Add the counterexample as a proposition. Remove
   "holds if the clean build's swings are bounded by N's own" from the TE4 comment and from 3-20. The next step is a
   proof under a new claim type whose error is term (c), what N sends through the values the map calls errors. With it,
   term (c) is bounded by that claim's error, and only terms (a) and (b), which carry B^0's swings, stay open.
2. **Make DE2 well defined and invariant under rescaling (findings 2 and 3).** Replace the Euclidean judgment projection
   by an equivariant clean value. Add "one judgment claim per region, a != 0" as a syntactic rule in DM4 or DM6.
   Restate TE4's judgment kappa.
3. **State TE4 with its true quantifiers and objects (findings 5, 6, 10).** U is a box of tables, not networks. The
   upper bound is an infimum over e and over e-near choices. The premise set is cover or flat. Fix the proposed trunk
   wording in 3-20 to match, before Shonu is asked to confirm R-wellposed.
