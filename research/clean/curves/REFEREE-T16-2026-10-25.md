## The three most important fixes, ranked

1. **Define J_k for every R, and give the closed form under a premise.** Define J_k(R) as the infimum in part 3 for
   every R with r'(R) <= k and finite second moments of u_R. Then part 4 (inequality, converse, equality of infima)
   holds with no positive-definite premise. Give the closed form when Cov(u_R) is positive definite. A weaker closed
   form also holds when Cov(u_R) is singular: put Y_R = C Sigma_R^{+1/2} (the pseudo-inverse; C vanishes on the
   kernel of Sigma_R) and F = Sigma_R^{1/2} col(R). Keep r'(R), not dim F, in the index of the sum: every column of R
   uses a slot of V, even a column in the kernel of Sigma_R. Add the one-line attainment Z = Y_R Pi_W Sigma^{-1/2}.
2. **Put the missing premises of part 5 in the statement and the tag.** "Assume C_f positive definite, k >= r and
   Cov(u) positive definite. Then r'(R*) = r and J_k(R*) is the value of T11 part 1." Add the finite moments of u_R
   and y to the preamble of T16.
3. **Restrict the Comment and D7 obstacle (3) to what is proved.** (a) "Two convex problems" only when n_y = 2 (one-dimensional
   centred logits), with r_1 = 1, and "infimum" over the open chambers. (b) Add "the columns of R* are independent" to the
   differentiability premises, and either prove the continuity of the part-4 build near R* or weaken "not a local
   minimum" to "not optimal". Replace "reduces to a search over R" by "the infimum equals an infimum over R".
