## The vertex cover argument, as the referee gave it

M = I_n, W_vv = L, W_uv = d on edges of Q, both orders, 1 on holes. S a least cover, I its complement: X = 1_I 1_I^T costs L tau on the diagonal, 0 on edges, at most n^2 on holes. Lower side: X = a b^T a least, cost at most L n. f_v = a_v b_v. On an edge uv, a_u^2 b_v^2 + a_v^2 b_u^2 >= 2 |f_u f_v|, so |f_u f_v| <= t^2 = L n / (2 d). So {v : f_v > t} is independent, its complement a cover, each member pays at least L (1 - t)^2.

## Exits it tried on the wall, which fail

Zeroing a row or column of a minimiser (uniform bound sqrt(d), circular, since d must be about f^2 and f grows like d^(k m)). Different weights for diagonal and adjacent pairs (same circle). Sign cells of hyperplanes (count depends on n). A random sample of columns (one column witnesses each conflict). TQ27 on a single color class (no gain). Whitening level by level (the column tail does not decay). Angular nets (same exponents). A PSD fit has no wall, and a free build is not PSD.
