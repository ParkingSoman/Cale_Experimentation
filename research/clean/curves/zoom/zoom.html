      What this shows about ResNet-18: its channels are not near-copies of each other, so the proved bound is far too large to use here, and down to about 100 channels merging is no better than pruning or random groups. From about 48 down to 16 channels the merges beat both. At 4 channels or fewer nothing works. Trained boxes do better at every size checked, so the rule is not the best possible build here.
