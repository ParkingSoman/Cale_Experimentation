**2. The measured answer to "why is the HP slow outside the model".** (`scripts/machine_probe.py`, results in `results/`.)

| | Mac | HP | HP slower by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internet | 28.4 MB/s | 28.9 MB/s | **1.0x** |
| One CPU thread | 0.088 s | 0.627 s | 7.1x |
| Create small files | 21,761 per s | 217 per s | **100x** |
| Read small files | 63,774 per s | 2,264 per s | 28x |

The HP runs two virus scanners (Windows Defender and McAfee) and has a fast NVMe disk. So the non-model gap is **CPU speed (3 to 7x) plus the cost of every file (12 to 100x)**, never the network. The cure is to touch far fewer files and to do less work at the moment a person waits.

**3. The fixed-time hardware test.** A reading is a window of time. It runs at least 2 s and until 32 answer tokens were seen, and never more than 7 s. The computer is brought to its working state first (short windows until two agree, at most three). Prompt lengths are set by time. Measured with `scripts/tune_check.py`:

| | Fixed work (before) | Fixed time (now) |
|---|---|---|
| Mac, the whole test | 131 s (12 s without the 120 s heat test) | **23 s, the same every run** |
| HP, the whole test (CPU engine) | 311 s | 64 to 119 s |
| Speed of each size against a slow, careful measurement | within 10 to 25% | Mac within 3 to 5%, HP within 6% on average |
| Spread between two runs, full size | 10 to 25% | Mac 1 to 2%, HP 5% |

A slower computer gets a less exact reading (fewer tokens in the window), never a longer test. The floor of 32 tokens exists because a 5 tokens/s laptop makes only 10 tokens in 2 s: the speed then varied by 32% between two runs.

## Traps

- `pkill -f "cale.chat"` also matches a real Cale chat. Stop test programs by exact path or by port.
- Before running any older test, check its default `--base` (`grep 47821`).
- The HP engine library at `C:\cale\lbuild2\b\cale_llama.dll` is old (no `cale_cancel`). Use `b_noomp8` (CPU) or `C:\cale\home_b3\lib\cale_llama.dll` (with CUDA and Vulkan).
- A hardware-test window on a slow computer holds few tokens. Do not shorten `MIN_TOKENS` without running `scripts/tune_check.py` on a slow computer.
- Shell scripts that contain `rm -rf` and are passed to `bash -c` are blocked by the safety check. Use fresh folder names instead.
- HP test folders were cleaned on 2026-10-18 (kept: `xb7`, `xb11`, `code44`, `venv`, `models`, `lbuild2`, `home_b3`, `jobs`). A chat bundle that was run by `chat_race.py` holds two junction links (`server\storage`, `collector\hotdir`): remove them (`cmd /c rmdir`) before using it as an install source.
